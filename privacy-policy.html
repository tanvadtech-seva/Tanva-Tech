1 Information We Collect

Tanva Tech is built with a privacy-first philosophy. Most of our products are designed to function without requiring you to create an account or provide personal information.

We do not intentionally collect or store the following through our Services unless explicitly stated:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Physical address
  • Profile photo
  • Government identification documents
  • Payment or banking information
  • Precise location information
  • Advertising identifiers for marketing purposes

Where a product requires certain information to function, that requirement will be clearly disclosed at the point of collection.

2 How We Use Information

Any information we do collect is used strictly to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our Services
  • Authenticate users when sign-in is required
  • Respond to inquiries and support requests
  • Improve the functionality and reliability of our products
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or selling to third parties.

3 Authentication & Sign-In

Some Tanva Tech products may offer optional sign-in methods such as Google Sign-In or other identity providers.

If you choose to use a third-party sign-in method, that provider may share basic account information necessary to authenticate you, such as an authentication identifier and basic profile details.

Important: Sign-in is optional. You are not required to authenticate to use features that do not require it.

Third-party identity providers process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage you to review their practices.

4 Camera, Photos & Permissions

Some of our products may request access to your device's camera, photo library, or other hardware features to provide specific functionality.

Such access is used only for the purpose disclosed at the time of the request. You control whether permissions are granted through your device's settings.

If you decline a permission, you may still use other parts of the product that do not depend on that permission.

5 Local Storage

Certain Tanva Tech products store data locally on your device — for example, documents, preferences, or settings.

Locally stored data is not intentionally uploaded to Tanva Tech servers unless explicitly stated in the product's documentation.

The availability and security of locally stored files depend on your device's operating system and storage protections. If you uninstall an app or clear its data, locally stored information may no longer be available.

6 Information Sharing

Tanva Tech does not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

We do not intentionally share personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or third-party marketing companies.

Information processed by third-party services you voluntarily use (such as a sign-in provider) is governed by those providers' own privacy policies.

We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal requests, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Tanva Tech, our users, or the public.

7 Third-Party Services

Our products may integrate with third-party services for functionality such as authentication, cloud storage, or analytics.

We aim to work only with providers who share our commitment to privacy. However, third-party services have their own terms and privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

Tanva Tech is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services you choose to use.

8 Children's Privacy

Our Services are intended for general audiences and are not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

9 Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through our Services.

Because some data may be stored locally on your device, you are also responsible for maintaining appropriate security on your device, including passwords, screen locks, operating system updates, and access controls.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. While we take reasonable precautions, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10 Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

Locally stored data remains on your device until you remove it or uninstall the relevant application.
